How it all got started.

In early 2022, Rhett and Adam got together and decided to write a musical. They wanted to cast kids alongside adults. Success was phenomenal. Encore Performing Arts developed and workshopped the show. People loved it. Now they're excited to share it with other theaters — it was a slam dunk for them, and it can be for you too.
